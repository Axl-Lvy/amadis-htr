\section{The corpus}
\label{sec:corpus}

Two bodies of sixteenth-century French print, not independent of one another.
\emph{Amadis de Gaule} is a chivalric romance cycle of \ocrBooks{} books,
\ocrPages{} scanned pages. The \emph{Trésor} anthologies reprint extracts from
that cycle under thematic headings. A \emph{Trésor} piece is therefore a
passage that already exists in the same corpus, which makes locating it a
retrieval task with a checkable answer.

The design specification treats both works as one target domain. Nothing below
is framed as in-domain against out-of-domain.

\subsection{Two editions, two type families}

The \ocrBooks{} books are not one edition. The first \ocrBooksFamilyA{}
(family~A) are a large folio setting in \emph{bâtarde} type, roman folio
numbers, each volume opening with a printed table of contents. The remaining
\ocrBooksFamilyB{} (family~B) are later and smaller: clean roman type, italic
rubrics, arabic page numbers, no table of contents. Family~A is
\ocrPagesFamilyA{} pages, family~B \ocrPagesFamilyB{}.

Three decisions follow. Recognition is reported per family. Structural
calibration covers family~A alone, because calibration needs a printed table of
contents to score against. Throughput pools both: every book was run by one
model on one device at one working width.

\subsection{Scan provenance}

Three holding institutions, three conditions. Book~1 is bitonal 600~dpi TIFFs
from the Bibliothèque municipale de Lyon; books~3 and~4 are greyscale Gallica
PDFs; book~18 is a Biblioteca Valenciana copy carrying that library's stamp,
which the layout stage suppresses as an illustration. The provenance of the
other volumes is recorded nowhere and is not claimed here.

Provenance is variation unrelated to the century, so
section~\ref{sec:protocol} stratifies on it. Reuse terms also differ per
institution and are unsettled for at least one, so no page image and no
transcribed text is committed: only counts, timings and offsets.

\subsection{The \emph{Trésor} pieces}

    observed range.}
  \label{fig:corpus-extracts}
\end{figure}

The reference is the editor's own catalogue,
\file{data/localisation/ground-truth.csv}: \corpusPieces{} pieces, each with a
Livre and, where the print gives one, a chapter. It is prose written by a
scholar, so the transcription keeps the hedging. \corpusCertain{} pieces
(\corpusCertainShare{}) carry an unqualified assignment; \corpusUncertain{}
are marked with a question mark, \corpusConjecture{} are bracketed conjectures,
\corpusUnassigned{} name no location. Only the certain band is scored.

The pieces draw on \corpusLivres{} of the \ocrBooks{} books. The matcher placed
\corpusAligned{} of them; figure~\ref{fig:corpus-extracts} gives the lengths,
median \corpusCharsMedian{} code points. The search space is the full
transcription of all \ocrBooks{} books, whose size in words no artefact here
records and which this report therefore does not state.

Two batches, kept apart everywhere. The workbook cohort is
\corpusPiecesWorkbook{} pieces from the editor's workbook, the batch that
existed when E5's rule was pre-registered. The catalogue cohort is
\corpusPiecesCatalogue{} pieces from the \emph{Juxtalinéaire} catalogue,
imported four days later. Pooling them would enlarge a test set after seeing its
numbers. The \corpusUnaligned{} pieces the matcher did not place sit in neither
cohort, which is why the two sum to \corpusAligned{} and not \corpusPieces{}.

\subsection{The training corpus}

\trainPages{} annotated pages, all from one Transkribus collection,
\emph{Trésor des Amadis}~T.1: \trainPagesTrain{} for training,
\trainPagesVal{} held out. The held-out pages are validation, not test --- they
selected the shipped checkpoint, and section~\ref{sec:limitations} states the
cost. The split is re-derived rather than inherited: the original file lists no
longer exist, and \file{docs/notes/2026-09-20-artefact-recovery.md} records the
recomputation and the seed control.

Two consequences. The training material is one
anthology volume, so both families of \emph{Amadis de Gaule} are largely unseen
by the model that transcribed them. And no \emph{Amadis} page is in the
training list --- page-level contamination is zero --- but the \emph{Trésor}
excerpts \emph{Amadis}, so the model has read some of the scored text in
another setting and another typeface. The report states this and does not
correct for it.
