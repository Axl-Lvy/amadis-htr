\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Each subsection opens with its claim, its unit of analysis, its baseline and
its uncertainty method, then reports. Experiments E1, E3 and E4 are not here:
they need an annotated gold set, which does not exist, and
section~\ref{sec:limitations} states what that costs.

\subsection{E5: locating a \emph{Trésor} extract}
\label{sec:e5}

\textbf{Claim.} A \emph{Trésor} extract can be located in the baseline text.
\textbf{Unit.} The piece, within a cohort. \textbf{Baseline.} None published;
the pre-registered gate stands in. \textbf{Uncertainty.} Bootstrap over pieces.

  \caption{E5, certain band only. The two cohorts are never pooled: the gate

Three denominators, and they are different. Pieces in the cohort; certain
pieces, for a Livre figure; and pieces whose printed chapter number is
recoverable, for a chapter figure --- \locChapterScoreableWorkbook{} of \locPiecesWorkbook{} on the gated cohort.
The remainder splits two ways: \locChapterUnavailableWorkbook{} pieces landed
on a printed label no chapter number could be decoded from, and
\locChapterUnreferencedWorkbook{} was catalogued to a Livre with no chapter
named, so it has no reference to score against.

Table~\ref{tab:e5} gives the result. Livre accuracy is
\locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{} on the workbook cohort and
\locLivreAccuracyCatalogue{} on the catalogue. Chapter accuracy is
\locChapterAccuracyWorkbook{} and \locChapterAccuracyCatalogue{}. The gate, written before
the matcher was scored, reads \qtr{Correct LIVRE well above 90\% means the
all-24-Livres search holds \dots{} Proceed to phase B}. The gated cohort is
\locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{}.

Figure~\ref{fig:e5-sweep} sweeps the accept threshold. Precision sits near

73\% and 53\%. The largest effect in E5 is the gap between Livre and chapter
accuracy.

\subsection{E2: the marker-anchor accept rule}
\label{sec:e2}

\textbf{Claim.} The accept rule keeps a generative pass from rewriting the
text. \textbf{Unit.} The line offered, clustered by page. \textbf{Baseline.}
Correction off, which is the frozen recognition output itself --- all
\ocrBooks{} books recorded zero words corrected. \textbf{Uncertainty.}
Stratified bootstrap over pages, 10,000 resamples, 95\%.

\textbf{Reach.} Over the whole corpus the pass would look at
\corrReachLinesWithSpan{} of \corrReachCorrectable{} correctable lines
(\corrReachLineShare{}), carrying \corrReachSpans{} suspect spans, on
\corrReachPagesWithSpan{} of \corrReachPages{} pages
(\corrReachPageShare{}). That count is deterministic: it is the recogniser's
own confidences, with no model involved.

\textbf{Sample.} \corrOffered{} lines on \corrPages{} pages, drawn 200 per type
family under a committed seed from the pages carrying at least one span.

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \input{generated/tables/e2-correction.tex}
  \caption{E2 by type family. The sample row carries counts only: the draw is
    equal per family and the frame is not, so a rate over it would weight
    family~A at more than twice its share. The corpus rate is frame-weighted.}
  \label{tab:e2}
\end{table}

In the sample, \corrAccepted{} of \corrOffered{} offered lines were accepted.
Weighted to the corpus's suspect lines, the acceptance rate is
\corrAcceptRate{} (95\%~CI \corrAcceptLow{} to \corrAcceptHigh{}, stratified
bootstrap over pages).

Of the refusals, \corrRewritesCaught{} were for changing text outside the
markers --- \corrRewriteRate{} of offered lines, frame-weighted, spanning
\corrRewriteSegments{} segments and \corrRewriteEdits{} characters, of which
\corrRewritesSubstantial{} moved more than two characters --- and
\corrProtocolFailures{} were replies that never became candidate edits at all.
Figure~\ref{fig:e2-reasons} gives the breakdown by branch, and
figure~\ref{fig:e2-confidence} the recogniser's confidence on accepted against
refused lines.

  \label{fig:e2-reasons}
\end{figure}

  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{generated/figures/e2-confidence.pdf}

\corrOnFindFallback{} of the offered lines (\corrFindFallbackShare{}) took the
span-locating fallback described in section~\ref{sec:errors}, on which an
acceptance is not evidence that the rule held.

The run used four concurrent workers, which the registered design assumed would
change throughput and not the verdict. Assumed is weaker than measured, so
\corrReplayLines{} lines on a seeded \corrReplayPages{}-page subsample were
replayed with one worker: \corrReplayAgree{} of \corrReplayLines{} verdicts
were identical. That is a small sample and not a proof of decode determinism,
but it is evidence where there was an assumption.

Acceptance is not an edit rate. Of the accepted lines,
\corrAcceptedUnchanged{} came back with every span untouched, and \corrChangedSpans{} spans were actually
altered.

\textbf{This is not an accuracy.} Scoring correction on against correction off
needs a reference transcription, which is the gold set, which does not exist.
E2 reports what the rule did and says nothing about whether the edits it kept
improve the text. The limit was registered before the run.

\textbf{Claim.} Descriptive only. \textbf{Unit.} Book and page.
\textbf{Baseline.} None. \textbf{Uncertainty.} None --- these are counts.

\ocrBooks{} books, \ocrPages{} pages, \ocrPagesFailed{} failures, in

  \centering

  \caption{Seconds per page by book.}
  \label{fig:e6-throughput}

\textbf{Structure is family~A alone, and hand-checked.} Books~13 to~24 print no
table of contents, so their calibration carries no match block and there is no
corpus-wide structural figure to report. On family~A the run recalls
\structMatchedA{} of \structChaptersA{} printed chapters
(\structRecallHandCheckedA{}) at a precision of
\structPrecisionHandCheckedA{}, missing \structMissingA{} and proposing
\structSpuriousA{} openings the printed contents do not carry.

Those two rates are not the detector unaided. Each book's corrections file
holds chapter decisions a reader made by hand, and calibration ran after them,
so what is reported is the corrected detector.
\file{docs/notes/2026-09-22-structural-figures-are-hand-checked.md} bounds the
difference. The counts beside them --- \structChaptersA{} printed chapters,
\structMatchedA{} matched --- are what they say they are.
