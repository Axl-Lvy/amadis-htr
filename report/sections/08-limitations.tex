\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Three of five experiments are unmeasured}

E1, E3 and E4 need an annotated gold set. Twenty pages at 20 to 40 minutes each
is roughly ten hours of one person's work, and it has not been done. The
consequence is specific and large: \textbf{claim~C1 is unevaluated}. This
report shows that the pipeline runs, how fast, what its accept rule refuses and
how well it locates a passage. It does not show that the fine-tune reads better
than the models it replaced.

E3 and E4 carry a further risk even once annotation exists. Below 30 instances
in a cell the analysis degrades to a descriptive breakdown, and some cells will
fall below it.

\subsection{The gold set is small and has one annotator}

Twenty pages, one annotator, with blind re-annotation of three pages as the
noise floor. That floor is the resolution limit: a difference smaller than one
person's disagreement with themselves is not a finding, and the report will not
claim one. A second annotator and an inter-annotator figure is the first thing
a successor should add.

\subsection{Validation is not test, and the split is one volume}

The \trainPagesVal{} held-out pages selected the shipped checkpoint. They are
validation, permanently, and no figure in this report treats them as a test
set. They were also split page-wise from a single volume, so the same formes
and the same type wear appear on both sides of the split --- a page-level split
of one book is a weaker guarantee than it looks.

\subsection{The model has read some of the text it is scored on}

No page of \emph{Amadis de Gaule} is in the training list, so page-level
contamination is zero. But the \emph{Trésor} excerpts \emph{Amadis}, and the
model trained on \emph{Trésor}~T.1. On \emph{Amadis} pages it has therefore
read some of the scored text before, in another setting and another typeface.
The report states this and does not correct for it, because the size of the
effect is not estimable without knowing which passages T.1 excerpts.

\subsection{Transkribus is a confounded baseline}

It segments independently and cannot be brought under the held-constant
segmentation. Any difference against it mixes recognition with layout. It is
reported as confounded rather than dropped, because dropping it would hide the
comparison a reader most wants; and its own output was searched for and not
found, so E1's fourth system may be absent entirely.

\subsection{E2 measures refusal, not improvement}

The accept rule's rejection rate says the rule works as specified. It says
nothing about whether the edits it accepted made the text better, because that
needs the gold set. E2 was registered with this limit written in
(\S\ref{sec:protocol}), and section~\ref{sec:e2} does not exceed it.

E2's sample is also \textbf{400 of the \corrReachPagesWithSpan{} pages}
carrying a suspect span, drawn under a committed
seed. It is a sample of the corpus, not the corpus.

\subsection{The structural figures are hand-checked}

Family~A's chapter recall and precision are the detector \emph{after} a reader
made 128 promotions and 217 demotions by hand. The unaided figure is not
recoverable from any artefact that survives, and
\file{docs/notes/2026-09-22-structural-figures-are-hand-checked.md} bounds its
recall as low as 77.0\% against the \structRecallHandCheckedA{} reported.

\subsection{Five of six thresholds are uncalibrated}

Table~\ref{tab:thresholds}. E5 sweeps \texttt{MIN\_SCORE}; the other five were
set by hand or left at a default and have never been swept, as were
\texttt{CHAIN\_GAP\_PENALTY} and \texttt{WINDOW\_SLACK}, which the
pre-registration names as preconditions for reading the chapter result. Any
result depending on one of them depends on a number nobody chose deliberately.

\subsection{Timings cover the batch runner only}

E6 measures the batch runner on one machine. The production orchestration runs
elsewhere, on hardware out of scope here, and its execution database was not
read. \ocrSecondsPerPage{}~s per page is this runner on this GPU and is not a
claim about the deployed system.
