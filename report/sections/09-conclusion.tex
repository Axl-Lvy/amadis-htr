\section{Conclusion and released artefacts}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Where the three claims stand}

\textbf{C1 is unevaluated.} The gold set does not exist, so the fine-tune's
advantage over the published models remains the untested premise it was at the
start of this work.

\textbf{C2 is supported on part of its evidence.} The rule behaves as specified
on the lines where the span-locating offset path held. On the
\corrOnFindFallback{} of \corrOffered{} lines that took the search fallback ---
\corrFindFallbackShare{} of them --- it was comparing against a line the
pipeline had already corrupted, so those acceptances carry no weight. The claim
therefore rests on under a third of the evidence gathered for it. Whether the accepted edits improve the text is a
further question, and needs C1's gold set.

\textbf{C3 is supported.} Livre accuracy is \locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{} on the
pre-registered cohort and \locLivreAccuracyCatalogue{} on the second, against a
gate that asked for above 90\%. Chapter accuracy is materially lower ---
\locChapterAccuracyWorkbook{} and \locChapterAccuracyCatalogue{}. The
pre-registration read that gap as span selection, not search, and named two
parameters to tune before concluding it; neither was tuned, so the reading
stands as an expectation confirmed in direction.

\subsection{What the evaluation changed}

\texttt{MIN\_SCORE} was checked rather than chosen. Precision is near 100\%
across the whole sweep, so the curve does not locate a threshold; what
figure~\ref{fig:e5-sweep} shows is that 0.808 sits at the top of the coverage
plateau, where nothing is lost. That is weaker than \emph{calibrated} and
stronger than \emph{inherited}. The other five constants in
table~\ref{tab:thresholds} are unswept and are now at least enumerated.

The evaluation also corrected the record. Four figures the project stated in
prose --- including a CER for the fine-tune with no test set, no log and no
code behind it --- were searched for, not substantiated, and dropped. They are
listed in \file{PROVENANCE.md}.

\subsection{Released artefacts}

\begin{itemize}
  \item this repository, holding the scoring code, the frozen run records and
    every result CSV, so each figure reruns from committed inputs with no GPU,
    no model and no network;
  \item \file{data/runs/ocr/}, the 24 books' run and calibration records;
  \item \file{data/runs/correction/}, E2's frame and its frozen verdicts;
  \item \file{data/runs/matcher/}, the frozen alignment export;
  \item the gold set and \file{data/gold/GUIDELINES.md}, written before
    annotation;
  \item the Hugging Face model card for \file{amadis-ft.mlmodel}, now writable
    because the shipped file is tied to its training checkpoint by weight
    comparison.
\end{itemize}

No page image and no transcribed text is released. Reuse terms differ per
holding institution and are unsettled for at least one, so what is published is
counts, timings, offsets and code.

\subsection{What a successor should do first}

Annotate the gold set. Everything blocked in this report is blocked behind it,
and it is ten hours of work rather than a research problem. Then add a second
annotator, so that the noise floor is a real inter-annotator figure instead of
one person's agreement with themselves.
