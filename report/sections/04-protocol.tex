\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\textbf{Units and uncertainty.} Each experiment resamples its own unit and
nothing smaller. For correction the unit is the line, grouped by page: lines on
the same page share a scan and the same worn type, so their outcomes are
correlated, and resampling single lines would give intervals that are too
narrow. For passage location the unit is the extract. Intervals are percentile
bootstrap intervals with 10{,}000 resamples at
95\%~\cite{efron1994bootstrap}. The training and throughput figures have no
interval, since each comes from a single run.

\textbf{Weighting the correction sample.} The sample takes the same number of
pages from each family, but the corpus does not hold them equally. Of its
\corrReachLinesWithSpan{} lines with a doubtful word, family~A has
\corrReachLinesWithSpanFamilyA{} and family~B \corrReachLinesWithSpanFamilyB{},
so a plain rate over the sample would give family~A much more weight than it
has in the corpus. Corpus-wide correction figures are therefore weighted by each
family's share of those lines, $r = w_A r_A + w_B r_B$, and the bootstrap
resamples within each family before combining them with the same weights. This
choice was made in writing before any result was scored.
