\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\textbf{Units and uncertainty.} Each experiment declares its own unit of
analysis and resamples that unit and no smaller one. E2's unit is the line
offered, clustered by the page: the lines on one page share a scan and a
stretch of type wear, so their verdicts are correlated and resampling lines
would report an interval narrower than the evidence supports. E5's is the
piece. Uncertainty is the percentile bootstrap, 10{,}000 resamples,
95\%~\cite{efron1994bootstrap}. E6 carries none, being counts.

\textbf{One weighting, fixed in advance.} E2 draws equally from two type
families that the corpus does not hold equally --- of its
\corrReachLinesWithSpan{} suspect lines, family~A carries
\corrReachLinesWithSpanFamilyA{} and family~B
\corrReachLinesWithSpanFamilyB{} --- so a rate summed over the sample would
weight family~A at more than twice its share. Every
pooled E2 figure is instead frame-weighted, $r = w_A r_A + w_B r_B$, with the
weights read from the deterministic reach census, and its interval is a
stratified bootstrap that resamples inside each family and recombines with the
same fixed weights. Which of the two arithmetics \qtr{pooled} meant was
settled in writing before any verdict was scored.

\textbf{Two pre-registrations.} E5's gate was written on 2026-09-15, before the
matcher was scored, and is quoted verbatim in
\file{docs/pre-registration/2026-09-15-e5-alignment-gate.md}. E2's design ---
the frame, the sample and its seed, the treatment, and the fixed list of
figures that would be reported --- was registered on 2026-09-22, before its
run. E2 is the one experiment here that can be re-run on demand, which is
exactly why its choices are dated earlier than its numbers. The same file
records the two runs started and discarded before the one that was scored, so
that what happened between the registration and the result is visible rather
than implied.

\textbf{What the protocol cannot do.} Scoring the correction pass against
correction off as a change in error rate needs a reference transcription for
the pages E2 sampled, and none exists: the corrected pages this repository
releases (section~\ref{sec:artefacts}) are \emph{Trésor} and one \emph{Amadis}
extract, while E2's frame is the \ocrBooks{} books' own OCR run. E2 therefore
reports what the accept rule did and makes no claim that the edits it kept are
improvements. That limit was registered in advance, not discovered afterwards.
