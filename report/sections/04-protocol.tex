\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

E1, E3 and E4 have not been run. What follows is the protocol they will run
under, fixed before the numbers exist; E2's and E5's, which have run, are
marked where they differ.

\textbf{The gold set.} The frame is every page of all \ocrBooks{} books of
\emph{Amadis de Gaule} minus any page in the training split. That list holds
\trainPages{} pages, all of them \emph{Trésor}~T.1, so the subtraction
currently removes nothing: page-level contamination is zero by construction,
not by luck. Strata are type family, scan provenance, and page kind restricted
to ordinary body pages --- front matter, tables of contents and woodcut pages
are layout problems, not recognition problems. The draw is seeded per stratum,
target 20 pages, which at 20 to 40 minutes a dense page is roughly ten hours of
annotation and is the binding constraint on this report. There is one
annotator; three pages are re-annotated blind after at least a week, and the
error rate between the two passes is the gold set's noise floor. \textbf{No
system difference smaller than that floor is claimed.} The transcription
convention is fixed in \file{data/gold/GUIDELINES.md}, committed before
annotation begins.

\textbf{Metrics.} Character and word error rate, defined in code rather than
prose: whitespace counts as a character, runs of whitespace collapse to one
space before comparison, and aggregation is character-weighted across pages.
Every figure is reported twice, raw and folded. The fold --- NFC,
long~\qtr{ſ} to \emph{s}, case folding, \emph{u}/\emph{v} and \emph{i}/\emph{j}
collapsed --- is declared once, applied identically to hypothesis and
reference, and is deliberately narrower than the matcher's own normaliser,
because anything it removed beyond a transcription convention would hide a real
recognition error. Uncertainty is the percentile bootstrap over pages, 10{,}000
resamples, 95\%~\cite{efron1994bootstrap}. Pages and not characters: a badly
inked or badly segmented page fails as a whole, so resampling characters would
report an interval narrower than the evidence supports.

\textbf{Baselines.} E1 will run the fine-tune against stock CATMuS-Print, a
chosen published early-modern model, and Transkribus, with segmentation held
constant across the first three. It cannot be held constant for Transkribus,
which segments independently, so that comparison mixes recognition with layout
and is reported as confounded.

\textbf{Two pre-registrations.} E5's gate was written on 2026-09-15, before the
matcher was scored, and is quoted verbatim in
\file{docs/pre-registration/2026-09-15-e5-alignment-gate.md}. E2's design ---
the frame, the sample and its seed, the treatment, and the fixed list of
figures that would be reported --- was registered on 2026-09-22, before its
run. E2 is the one experiment here that could be re-run on demand, which is
exactly why its choices are dated earlier than its numbers.

\textbf{What the protocol cannot do.} Scoring the correction pass against
correction off as a change in error rate needs a reference transcription. The
gold set does not exist, so E2 reports what the accept rule did and makes no
claim that the edits it kept are improvements. That limit was registered in
advance, not discovered afterwards.
