\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

E1, E3 and E4 have not been run. What follows is the protocol they will run
under, fixed before the numbers exist; E2's and E5's, which have run, are
marked where they differ.

\textbf{The gold set.} \goldPages{} pages carrying a corrected transcription:
\goldPagesTresor{} of \emph{Trésor}~T.1 and \goldPagesAmadis{} of \emph{Amadis
de Gaule} livre~13, \goldLines{} lines and \goldChars{} characters. They were
not annotated for this report; they were corrected in Transkribus in the course
of the edition. \file{eval/build\_gold\_set.py} selects them from
\goldCandidates{} candidates: a page qualifies only if a human marked it
finished and it is absent from the training split. That removes
\goldRefusedTrained{} pages, \goldRefusedUncorrected{} carry no correction at
all, and \goldRefusedExcluded{} is front matter. \file{data/gold/MANIFEST.csv}
gives a row per candidate with its reason, so the set is checkable rather than
asserted. The convention is \file{data/gold/GUIDELINES.md}, committed first.

This replaces the twenty pages drawn seeded per stratum and annotated blind
that this section first specified: ten hours of annotation was the binding
constraint, and \goldPages{} already-corrected pages cost none of it. Four
things were traded for that. The reference is \emph{post-edited, not blind} ---
each page was corrected over a model's own output, which the manifest records
per page --- so an error that reads plausibly survives, and the set leans
towards the fine-tune, the system E1 exists to test. Its segmentation is
\emph{Transkribus's own}, which makes that comparison worse than the confounded
one below. Its frame is \emph{one work and one copy}, so the family and
provenance strata collapse to a single cell. And \emph{no page was transcribed
twice}, so there is no noise floor and \textbf{no difference is yet small
enough to refuse.} Three pages transcribed blind under the same convention
would settle the floor and size the lean at once.

\textbf{Metrics.} Character and word error rate, defined in code rather than
prose: whitespace counts as a character, runs of whitespace collapse to one
space before comparison, and aggregation is character-weighted across pages.
Every figure is reported twice, raw and folded. The fold --- NFC,
long~\qtr{ſ} to \emph{s}, case folding, \emph{u}/\emph{v} and \emph{i}/\emph{j}
collapsed --- is declared once, applied identically to hypothesis and
reference, and is deliberately narrower than the matcher's own normaliser,
because anything it removed beyond a transcription convention would hide a real
recognition error. Uncertainty is the percentile bootstrap over pages, 10{,}000
resamples, 95\%~\cite{efron1994bootstrap}. Pages and not characters: a badly
inked or badly segmented page fails as a whole, so resampling characters would
report an interval narrower than the evidence supports.

\textbf{Baselines.} E1 will run the fine-tune against stock CATMuS-Print, a
chosen published early-modern model, and Transkribus, with segmentation held
constant across the first three. It cannot be held constant for Transkribus,
which segments independently, so that comparison mixes recognition with layout
and is reported as confounded.

\textbf{Two pre-registrations.} E5's gate was written on 2026-09-15, before the
matcher was scored, and is quoted verbatim in
\file{docs/pre-registration/2026-09-15-e5-alignment-gate.md}. E2's design ---
the frame, the sample and its seed, the treatment, and the fixed list of
figures that would be reported --- was registered on 2026-09-22, before its
run. E2 is the one experiment here that could be re-run on demand, which is
exactly why its choices are dated earlier than its numbers.

\textbf{What the protocol cannot do.} Scoring the correction pass against
correction off as a change in error rate needs a reference transcription for
the pages E2 sampled. The gold set does not cover them --- it is \emph{Trésor}
and one \emph{Amadis} extract, while E2's frame is the \ocrBooks{} books' own
OCR run --- so E2 reports what the accept rule did and makes no claim that the
edits it kept are improvements. That limit was registered in advance, not
discovered afterwards.
