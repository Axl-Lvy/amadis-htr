\section{Conclusion and released artefacts}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\claim{C1}{is unevaluated.} The gold set does not exist, so the fine-tune's
advantage over the published models remains the untested premise it was at the
start of this work.

\claim{C2}{is supported on part of its evidence.} The rule behaves as specified
on the lines where the span-locating offset path held. On the
\corrOnFindFallback{} of \corrOffered{} lines that took the search fallback it
was comparing against a line the pipeline had already corrupted, so those
acceptances carry no weight, and the claim rests on under a third of the
evidence gathered for it. Whether the accepted edits improve the text is a
further question, and needs C1's gold set.

\claim{C3}{is supported.} Livre accuracy is \locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{} on the
pre-registered cohort and \locLivreAccuracyCatalogue{} on the second, against a
gate that asked for above 90\%. Chapter accuracy is materially lower ---
\locChapterAccuracyWorkbook{} and \locChapterAccuracyCatalogue{} --- and the
pre-registration read that gap as span selection, naming two parameters to tune
before concluding it. Neither was tuned, so the reading stands as an
expectation confirmed in direction.

\subsection{What the evaluation changed}

\texttt{MIN\_SCORE} was checked rather than chosen. Precision is near 100\%
across the whole sweep, so the curve does not locate a threshold; what
figure~\ref{fig:e5-sweep} shows is that 0.808 sits at the top of the coverage
plateau, where nothing is lost. That is weaker than \emph{calibrated} and
stronger than \emph{inherited}. The other five constants in
table~\ref{tab:thresholds} are unswept and are now at least enumerated.

The evaluation also corrected the record. Four figures the project stated in
prose --- including a CER for the fine-tune with no test set, no log and no
code behind it --- were searched for, not substantiated, and dropped. They are
listed in \file{PROVENANCE.md}.

\subsection{Released artefacts}

This repository holds the scoring code, the frozen run records and every result
CSV, so each figure reruns from committed inputs with no GPU, no model and no
network: \file{data/runs/ocr/} for the \ocrBooks{} books' run and calibration
records, \file{data/runs/correction/} for E2's frame and its frozen verdicts,
\file{data/runs/matcher/} for the alignment export, and
\file{data/gold/GUIDELINES.md}, written before annotation. The Hugging Face
model card for \file{amadis-ft.mlmodel} is now writable, because the shipped
file is tied to its training checkpoint by weight comparison.

Beyond the illustrative plates in section~\ref{sec:corpus}, no page image and
no transcribed text is released. Reuse terms differ per holding institution and
are unsettled for at least one, so what is published is counts, timings,
offsets and code.

\subsection{What a successor should do first}

Annotate the gold set. Everything blocked in this report is blocked behind it,
and it is ten hours of work rather than a research problem. Then fix the
span-locating fallback, which currently costs C2 two thirds of its evidence.
