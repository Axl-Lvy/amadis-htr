\subsection{E5: locating a \emph{Trésor} extract}
\label{sec:e5}

\textbf{Claim.} A \emph{Trésor} extract can be located in the baseline text.
\textbf{Unit.} The piece, within a cohort. \textbf{Baseline.} None published;
the pre-registered gate stands in. \textbf{Uncertainty.} Bootstrap over pieces.

Three denominators, and they differ: pieces in the cohort, certain pieces for a
Livre figure, and pieces whose printed chapter number is recoverable for a
chapter figure --- \locChapterScoreableWorkbook{} of \locPiecesWorkbook{} on
the gated cohort. Of the remainder, \locChapterUnavailableWorkbook{} pieces
landed on a printed label no chapter number could be decoded from and
\locChapterUnreferencedWorkbook{} was catalogued to a Livre with no chapter
named.

Table~\ref{tab:e5} gives the result: Livre accuracy \locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{}
on the workbook cohort and \locLivreAccuracyCatalogue{} on the catalogue,
chapter accuracy \locChapterAccuracyWorkbook{} and
\locChapterAccuracyCatalogue{}. The gate, written before the matcher was
scored, reads \qtr{Correct LIVRE well above 90\% means the all-24-Livres search
holds}.

\begin{figure}[t]

  \caption{E5 accept threshold against precision (top) and coverage (bottom).
    Thresholds accepting nothing are omitted, precision over an empty accept

Figure~\ref{fig:e5-sweep} sweeps the accept threshold. Precision sits near
100\% at every threshold in both cohorts, so the curve does not locate an
operating point; coverage does. Coverage holds above 98\% to 0.86 on the
workbook cohort and to 0.85 on the catalogue, then falls away: by 0.90 it is
73\% and 53\%. The largest effect in E5 is the gap between Livre and chapter
accuracy.

\subsection{E2: the marker-anchor accept rule}
\label{sec:e2}

\textbf{Claim.} The accept rule keeps a generative pass from rewriting the
text. \textbf{Unit.} The line offered, clustered by page. \textbf{Baseline.}
Correction off, which is the frozen recognition output itself --- all
\ocrBooks{} books recorded zero words corrected. \textbf{Uncertainty.}
Stratified bootstrap over pages, 10{,}000 resamples, 95\%.

Over the whole corpus the pass would look at \corrReachLinesWithSpan{} of
\corrReachCorrectable{} correctable lines (\corrReachLineShare{}), carrying
\corrReachSpans{} suspect spans, on \corrReachPagesWithSpan{} of
\corrReachPages{} pages (\corrReachPageShare{}). That count is deterministic:
it is the recogniser's own confidences, with no model involved. The sample is
\corrOffered{} lines on \corrPages{} pages, drawn 200 per type family under a
committed seed.

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \input{generated/tables/e2-correction.tex}
  \caption{E2 by type family. The sample row carries counts only: the draw is
    equal per family and the frame is not, so a rate over it would weight
    family~A at more than twice its share. The corpus rate is frame-weighted.}
  \label{tab:e2}
\end{table}

\corrAccepted{} of \corrOffered{} offered lines were accepted; weighted to the
corpus's suspect lines the acceptance rate is \corrAcceptRate{} (95\%~CI
\corrAcceptLow{} to \corrAcceptHigh{}). Of the refusals, \corrRewritesCaught{}
were for changing text outside the markers --- \corrRewriteRate{} of offered
lines, frame-weighted, spanning \corrRewriteSegments{} segments and
\corrRewriteEdits{} characters, of which \corrRewritesSubstantial{} moved more
than two characters. The other \corrProtocolFailures{} refusals were replies
that never became candidate edits at all, and bear on the claim not at all.
Figure~\ref{fig:e2-confidence} gives the recogniser's confidence on accepted
against refused lines.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{generated/figures/e2-confidence.pdf}
  \caption{Lowest character confidence on a line, accepted against refused.}
  \label{fig:e2-confidence}
\end{figure}

\corrOnFindFallback{} of the offered lines (\corrFindFallbackShare{}) took the
span-locating fallback described in section~\ref{sec:errors}, on which an
acceptance is not evidence that the rule held.

The run used four concurrent workers, which the registered design assumed would
change throughput and not the verdict; replaying \corrReplayLines{} lines from a
seeded \corrReplayPages{}-page subsample with one worker reproduced
\corrReplayAgree{} of \corrReplayLines{} verdicts. That is evidence where there
was an assumption, not a proof of decode determinism.

Acceptance is not an edit rate: of the accepted lines,
\corrAcceptedUnchanged{} came back with every span untouched, and
\corrChangedSpans{} spans were actually altered. \textbf{And this is not an
accuracy.} Scoring correction on against correction off needs a reference
transcription for the pages E2 sampled, and none exists. E2 reports what the rule
did and says nothing about whether the edits it kept improve the text.

\subsection{E6: throughput and structure}
\label{sec:e6}

\textbf{Claim.} Descriptive only. \textbf{Unit.} Book and page.
\textbf{Baseline.} None. \textbf{Uncertainty.} None --- these are counts.

\ocrBooks{} books, \ocrPages{} pages, \ocrPagesFailed{} failures, in
\ocrHours{} hours at \ocrSecondsPerPage{}~s per page.
Figure~\ref{fig:e6-throughput} gives the per-book spread; the families differ
little, \ocrSecondsPerPageFamilyA{}~s against \ocrSecondsPerPageFamilyB{}~s.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{generated/figures/e6-throughput.pdf}
  \caption{Seconds per page by book.}
  \label{fig:e6-throughput}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Structure is family~A alone, and hand-checked.} Books~13 to~24 print no
table of contents, so their calibration carries no match block and there is no
corpus-wide structural figure. On family~A the run recalls \structMatchedA{} of
\structChaptersA{} printed chapters (\structRecallHandCheckedA{}) at a precision
of \structPrecisionHandCheckedA{}, missing \structMissingA{} and proposing
\structSpuriousA{} openings the printed contents do not carry. Those two rates
are not the detector unaided: each book's corrections file holds chapter
decisions a reader made by hand, and calibration ran after them, so what is
reported is the corrected detector. The counts beside them are what they say
they are.
