\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

E1, E3 and E4 have not been run. What follows is the protocol they will run
under, fixed before the numbers exist; E2's and E5's, which have run, are
marked where they differ.

\subsection{The gold set}

\textbf{Frame.} Every page of all \ocrBooks{} books of \emph{Amadis de Gaule},
minus any page in \file{data/gold/splits/training-pages.csv}. That list holds
\trainPages{} pages, all of them \emph{Trésor}~T.1, so the subtraction
currently removes nothing --- which is a fact worth stating rather than a
technicality, because it means page-level contamination is zero by
construction and not by luck.

\textbf{Strata.} Type family (section~\ref{sec:corpus}); scan provenance,
with the stamped Biblioteca Valenciana copy as its own stratum; and page kind,
restricted to ordinary body pages. Front matter, tables of contents, privilege
leaves and pages dominated by a woodcut are excluded: those are layout
problems, not recognition problems.

\textbf{Draw.} Seeded, per stratum, by \file{src/amadis_htr/sampling.py}; a
manifest records why each page is in the set. Target 20 pages. At 20 to 40
minutes a dense page that is roughly ten hours of annotation, which is the
binding constraint on this report.

\textbf{Annotator.} One. Three pages are re-annotated blind after at least a
week, and the error rate between the two passes is the gold set's own noise
floor. \textbf{No system difference smaller than that floor is claimed.}

\textbf{Convention.} \file{data/gold/GUIDELINES.md}, written and committed
before annotation begins. It fixes a normalised transcription matching the
convention the model was trained to emit --- modern \emph{j} and \emph{v},
long~\qtr{ſ} transcribed \emph{s}, abbreviations resolved, drop caps spelled
out, accents as the compositor set them, and spelling never modernised.

\subsection{Metrics}

Character and word error rate, with the definition pinned in code rather than
described in prose: whitespace counts as a character, runs of whitespace
collapse to one space before comparison, and aggregation is character-weighted
across pages.

Every figure is reported twice, raw and folded. The fold is declared once, in
\file{src/amadis_htr/fold.py}, and applied identically to hypothesis and
reference: NFC, long~\qtr{ſ} to \emph{s}, case folding, \emph{u}/\emph{v}
collapsed, \emph{i}/\emph{j} collapsed. It is deliberately narrower than the
matcher's own normaliser, because anything it removed beyond a transcription
convention would hide a real recognition error.

\subsection{Uncertainty}

Percentile bootstrap over pages, 10,000 resamples, 95\%~\cite{efron1994bootstrap}.
Pages and not characters: a badly inked or badly segmented page fails as a
whole, so its errors are correlated and resampling characters would report an
interval narrower than the evidence supports.

\subsection{Baselines, and one that is confounded}

E1 will run the fine-tune against \texttt{stock} CATMuS-Print, a chosen
published early-modern model, and Transkribus --- four systems. Segmentation is
held constant across the first three. It cannot be for Transkribus, which
segments independently, so that comparison mixes recognition with layout and is
reported as confounded.

\subsection{Two pre-registrations}

E5's gate was written in the private repository on 2026-09-15, before the
matcher was scored, and is quoted verbatim in
\file{docs/pre-registration/2026-09-15-e5-alignment-gate.md}. Its source file
is untracked, and the only timestamp it carried before this repository was a
filesystem mtime.

E2's design was registered on 2026-09-22, before its run, in
\file{docs/pre-registration/2026-09-22-e2-correction-design.md}: the frame, the
sample and its seed, the treatment, and the fixed list of figures that would be
reported. E2 is the one experiment here that could be re-run on demand, which
is exactly why its choices are dated earlier than its numbers.

\subsection{What the protocol cannot do}

Scoring the correction pass against correction off as a change in error rate
needs a reference transcription. The gold set does not exist yet, so E2 reports
what the accept rule did and makes no claim that the edits it kept are
improvements. That limit was registered in advance, not discovered afterwards.
