\section{Corpus and system}
\label{sec:corpus}

Two bodies of sixteenth-century French print, not independent of one another.
\emph{Amadis de Gaule} is a chivalric romance cycle of \ocrBooks{} books,
\ocrPages{} scanned pages. The \emph{Trésor} anthologies reprint extracts from
that cycle under thematic headings, so locating a \emph{Trésor} piece is a
retrieval task with a checkable answer. The design specification treats both
works as one target domain; nothing below is framed as in-domain against
out-of-domain.

\subsection{Two editions, three provenances}

The \ocrBooks{} books are not one edition. The first \ocrBooksFamilyA{}
(family~A) are a large folio setting in \emph{bâtarde} type with roman folio
numbers, each volume opening with a printed table of contents; the remaining
\ocrBooksFamilyB{} (family~B) are later and smaller, in clean roman type with
italic rubrics and arabic page numbers and no table of contents. Family~A is
\ocrPagesFamilyA{} pages, family~B \ocrPagesFamilyB{}. Recognition is therefore
reported per family, structural calibration covers family~A alone --- it needs
a printed table of contents to score against --- and throughput pools both,
because every book was run by one model on one device at one working width.

Three holding institutions, three conditions: book~1 is bitonal 600~dpi TIFFs
from the Bibliothèque municipale de Lyon, books~3 and~4 greyscale Gallica PDFs,
and book~18 a Biblioteca Valenciana copy carrying that library's stamp. The
provenance of the other volumes is recorded nowhere and is not claimed here.
Provenance is variation unrelated to the century, so
section~\ref{sec:protocol} stratifies on it.

\subsection{The \emph{Trésor} pieces, and the training corpus}

The reference is the editor's own catalogue,
\file{data/localisation/ground-truth.csv}: \corpusPieces{} pieces, each with a
Livre and, where the print gives one, a chapter. It is prose written by a
scholar, so the transcription keeps the hedging. \corpusCertain{} pieces
(\corpusCertainShare{}) carry an unqualified assignment; \corpusUncertain{} are
marked with a question mark, \corpusConjecture{} are bracketed conjectures,
\corpusUnassigned{} name no location. Only the certain band is scored. The
matcher placed \corpusAligned{} pieces drawing on \corpusLivres{} of the
\ocrBooks{} books, median \corpusCharsMedian{} code points and longest
\corpusCharsMax{}.

Two batches, kept apart everywhere: the workbook cohort is
\corpusPiecesWorkbook{} pieces, the batch that existed when E5's rule was
pre-registered, and the catalogue cohort \corpusPiecesCatalogue{} pieces
imported four days later. Pooling them would enlarge a test set after seeing
its numbers.

The training corpus is \trainPages{} annotated pages, all from one Transkribus
collection, \emph{Trésor des Amadis}~T.1: \trainPagesTrain{} for training,
\trainPagesVal{} held out. The held-out pages are validation, not test --- they
selected the shipped checkpoint --- and both families of \emph{Amadis de Gaule}
are therefore largely unseen by the model that transcribed them.
Section~\ref{sec:limitations} states what that split costs.

\subsection{The pipeline}
\label{sec:system}

  \centering

  \includegraphics[width=0.33\linewidth]{plates/spec-dropcap.png}\hfill
  \includegraphics[width=0.60\linewidth]{plates/spec-header.png}
  \caption{What the layout stage looks at, all of it geometry. Top: body sits
    near $0^\circ$ and this rubric runs 12--18$^\circ$, measured against the
    page's own median body slant, so slant marks a title and wording never
    does. Bottom left: an ink blob 1.6--9$\times$ the line height, aspect ratio
    0.4--1.8, in the column's left 20\% is a drop cap. Bottom right: a short
    centred line with a folio number is a running head.}
  \label{fig:layout}
\end{figure}

Recognition is kraken~7.0.2 \cite{kiessling2025kraken} with a model fine-tuned
from CATMuS-Print [Large] \cite{gabay2024catmusprintlarge} on the \trainPages{}
pages above. The shipped artefact is \file{amadis-ft.mlmodel}; its SHA-256 is
in \file{PROVENANCE.md}, and weight comparison ties it to the training
checkpoint that produced it. Two configurations were run: the shipped one uses
a reduce-on-plateau schedule at learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$ with
augmentation, and an earlier constant-rate run at $10^{-3}$ was not shipped.

Layout is deterministic and carries no learned component
(figure~\ref{fig:layout}): baseline bands give reading order, oversize or
italic-slanted lines become titles, lines in the top and bottom margins become
headers and footers, and illustration regions and library stamps are suppressed
rather than read. What it cannot settle is the ambiguous case --- a single line
at the top or foot of a page that may be a running head, a catchword, or the
first or last line of the body. That residue is what E3 would score.

Two LLM passes sit behind the recogniser. Both are opt-in, both run after
recognition, and both degrade to \emph{change nothing} when the model is absent
or fails. The \textbf{coherence pass} resolves an ambiguous head or foot line
by asking whether removing it lets the neighbouring lines read continuously.
The \textbf{low-confidence pass} is what claim~C2 is about: every word
containing a character scored below \texttt{LOWCONF\_THRESHOLD} is wrapped in
\lmark~\rmark{} and the line is sent with its two neighbours for context, the
prompt forbidding modernised spelling. The reply is split on the markers and
accepted only if the marker count matches and every segment \emph{outside} the
markers is byte identical to the original. Otherwise the entire line reverts;
there is no partial acceptance. That rule is the substance of the contribution:
it makes a rewrite structurally detectable instead of plausible, so it can be
refused.

Localisation is retrieval over the full transcription of all \ocrBooks{} books:
a period-aware normaliser folds the orthographic variation that would defeat
exact matching, a rare-word seed index --- words of at least
\texttt{MIN\_SEED\_LENGTH} characters with document frequency at most
\texttt{MAX\_SEED\_DF} --- proposes candidate positions, and per-book anchor
chaining assembles them into a span accepted above \texttt{MIN\_SCORE}.
