\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:errors}
% Two labels for one section, so that references to either still land here.
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Correction}

The warning from the literature is that an LLM corrector rewrites text it
should leave alone~\cite{kanerva2025nofreelunches, ehrmann2026hipeocrepair}.
The accept rule makes such rewrites visible. The \corrRewritesCaught{} refused
replies changed text outside the marked words, and a rule without markers would
have kept them without anyone noticing. The rule is strict, though. It refuses
any difference at all, so a moved space counts the same as a rewritten clause,
and only \corrRewritesSubstantial{} of the \corrRewritesCaught{} changed more
than two characters.

\textbf{Kept does not mean better.} Knowing whether the kept edits improve the
text needs a reference transcription of the sampled pages, and there is none.
The sample is also \corrPages{} of the \corrReachPagesWithSpan{} pages with a
doubtful word, so its figures are estimates for the corpus.

\subsection{What is not measured}

\textbf{Recognition on unseen pages.} The fine-tune has a validation curve and
nothing more. This report does not show how well the model reads pages it has
never seen, and does not compare it with the published models it replaced. The
usual confusions, long~\qtr{ſ} for \qtr{f},
\qtr{u} for \qtr{n} and \qtr{c} for \qtr{e}, have not been counted. The
corrected pages released with this report (appendix~\ref{app:data}) are a
starting point, with known limits: they were corrected from a model's output
rather than transcribed from scratch, by a single person, from one work and one
copy, and they keep Transkribus's line segmentation.

\textbf{Validation is not test.} The \trainCer{} comes from \trainPagesVal{}
pages that were also used to choose the checkpoint. They were split page by
page from the same volume as the training pages, so they share its type and its
wear. The figure is therefore somewhat optimistic for the \emph{Trésor}, and it
says nothing direct about \emph{Amadis de Gaule}. The \emph{Trésor} also
reprints passages of \emph{Amadis}, so the model has already seen some of the
cycle's text, to an extent that cannot be estimated without knowing which
passages T.1 contains.

\textbf{Settings and timings.} Apart from the location accept threshold, none
of the settings listed in section~\ref{sec:system} was tuned, including the two
chaining settings that decide where a span starts and ends. The timings
describe the batch runner on one GPU, not the deployed system.
