\begin{abstract}
% Written from the measured figures only. Every number here also appears in
% section 6; nothing is introduced by the abstract.
A production pipeline has been transcribing a \ocrBooks{}-book cycle of
16th-century French romance for a year on the strength of one validation error
rate, measured on a 10\% page-level split of a single volume. This report
measures what that number cannot support.

Of the five experiments that test a claim, two are reported and three are not;
a sixth, descriptive, is. The correction pass's marker-anchor accept rule ---
suspect spans wrapped in \lmark~\rmark{}, a reply accepted only when every byte
outside the markers returns unchanged --- was run over \corrOffered{}
low-confidence lines on a seeded \corrPages{}-page sample of the
\corrReachPagesWithSpan{} eligible pages, under a design registered beforehand.
Weighted to the corpus it accepted \corrAcceptRate{} (95\%~CI \corrAcceptLow{}
to \corrAcceptHigh{}) and refused \corrRewritesCaught{} replies for changing
text outside the markers. That branch is what the rule claims to catch; it
fires on any byte difference, so it bounds fluent rewriting from above rather
than counting it.
Passage localisation reaches \locLivreAccuracyWorkbook{} Livre accuracy on the
pre-registered cohort against a gate written before the numbers existed, and
\locChapterAccuracyWorkbook{} chapter accuracy --- a gap the pre-registration
had already read as span selection, and whose two named preconditions for that
reading were never tuned. The batch runner
transcribed \ocrPages{} pages with \ocrPagesFailed{} failures at
\ocrSecondsPerPage{}~s per page.

Recognition itself is unevaluated: it needs a 20-page annotated gold set that
does not exist, and this report names the absence instead of substituting a
figure it can compute. Measuring the accept rule also exposed a hole in it.
\corrFindFallbackShare{} of the offered lines took a span-locating fallback
that can mark one word twice, and on those an acceptance is not evidence that
the rule held --- so the claim rests on under a third of its own evidence. The scoring code, the frozen run records and every result CSV are
released --- no page image, no transcribed text --- so each figure rebuilds
from committed inputs with no GPU, no model and no network.
\end{abstract}
