\begin{frame}{The problem}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \ocrBooks{} books, \ocrPages{} pages of 16th-century French romance
    \item One year of pipeline, one recognition figure: a validation CER on a
      10\% split of one volume
    \item The correction pass and the retrieval stage behind it: never
      evaluated at all
  \end{itemize}
  \vfill
  This is the measurement of those two.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What got measured}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{E2} correction --- the accept rule, on \corrOffered{} lines
      from a seeded \corrPages{}-page sample
    \item \textbf{E5} localisation --- \locPiecesWorkbook{} pieces under the
      pre-registered gate, plus \locPiecesCatalogue{} imported later and
      reported beside them, never pooled
    \item \textbf{E6} throughput and structure --- \ocrPages{} pages
  \end{itemize}
  \vfill
  Two claims tested, both pre-registered. Recognition accuracy is not in
  scope.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Recognition accuracy is not measured}, here or anywhere
      else in the project.
    \item The structural rates are hand-checked, and named for it.
    \item Five of six thresholds have never been swept.
    \item E2 measures refusal, not improvement.
    \item \textbf{The accept rule has a hole}, and
      \corrFindFallbackShare{} of E2's lines sat on it --- a span-locating
      fallback that can mark one word twice, which the rule cannot detect. C2
      rests on under a third of its own evidence.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the evaluation changed}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{MIN\_SCORE} checked rather than chosen: 0.808 sits at the
      top of the coverage plateau. The other five constants are unswept and
      now at least enumerated.
    \item Four figures the project stated in prose --- including a CER for
      the fine-tune with no test set, no log and no code --- searched for,
      not substantiated, dropped.
    \item A hole in the accept rule, found by measuring it, with the fix
      named.
  \end{itemize}
  \vfill
  Everything here reruns from committed inputs: no GPU, no model, no network.
\end{frame}
